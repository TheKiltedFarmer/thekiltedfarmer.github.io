Privacy Notes

What the Hub keeps, why, and who can see it.

Open the Hub Help Terms Privacy

Last updated October 19, 2026

The short version: your business’s information is yours. We keep it only so the Hub can work for you. We never sell it, never show ads, and you can download all of it or ask us to delete it whenever you want.

What we keep

  • Your login: your email address and a password (stored scrambled; we can’t see it).
  • What you put in: sales, expenses, receipt photos, bank statement lines you upload, hours, schedules, pay settings, invoices and the customer details on them, inventory, mileage, and new-hire paperwork checklists.
  • Team information: names, emails, roles and pay details you or your team enter.
  • Billing: whether your plan is paid and when it renews. Stripe handles cards; we never see or store card numbers.
  • Watch alerts and Bid & grant finder: the topics and line of work you ask us to watch for.

Social Security numbers, bank account numbers and ID documents don’t belong in the Hub. The onboarding checklist tracks that paperwork was done, not the paperwork itself. Keep those originals somewhere secure, or with your payroll service.

Where it lives

Receipt photos are stored privately. They open only for people in your business who can see expenses, through a link that expires after a few minutes.

Your data is stored with Supabase, a database service,, encrypted while it travels and while it’s stored. Each business’s data is walled off from every other business at the database level, so only the people you let into your business can see it.

Who can see it

  • You and the people you invite. Owners and managers see the business’s books and pay. Team members see their own hours, schedule and pay, plus whatever the owner shares.
  • Us, only when needed: to fix a problem you ask us to look at, to keep the service secure, or when the law requires it.
  • Service providers who run parts of the Hub for us: Supabase (storage and sign-in), Stripe (payments), and GitHub (hosting the web pages). They can only use your data to provide that service.

We don’t sell or rent your data, we don’t share it with advertisers, and we don’t use trackers or ad cookies. The Hub remembers your sign-in on your device so you don’t have to log in every time.

How long we keep it

For as long as your Hub is open. If you cancel, we keep it for 12 months after your Hub pauses so you can come back or download it, then we delete it. Backups roll off within 30 days after that. Ask, and we’ll delete it sooner.

Your choices

  • Download everything: Settings → Your Plan → Download all my data.
  • Fix anything: edit it right in the Hub.
  • Delete your business or your login: email hello@thepeoplesvoices.org from the email on your account. We’ll confirm, and it’s gone within 30 days.

If something goes wrong

If we ever learn that someone got into data they shouldn’t have, we’ll tell the affected business owners promptly and explain what happened and what we’re doing about it, as Michigan law requires.

Changes

If we change these notes in a way that matters, we’ll email business owners first. Questions any time: hello@thepeoplesvoices.org.
