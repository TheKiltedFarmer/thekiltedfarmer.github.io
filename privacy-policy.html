What we collect

We collect your email address, your ZIP code, your display name (which can be a pseudonym), and your activity on the platform: votes, comments, ideas, and projects you engage with.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your street address, phone number, Social Security number, financial information, or any government-issued ID. We do not track your browsing history or use third-party advertising trackers.

How your data is used

Your data is used only to run The People's Voices: to confirm you live in the Michigan county you join, to show your participation in the community, and to send platform notifications if you opt in. Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for commercial purposes.

Who can see your information

Your display name and public activity (votes marked public, comments, ideas) are visible to other signed-in members. Your email address is never displayed publicly. "Anonymous" votes are not displayed publicly, but they are stored in our database with your account to prevent fraud.

Neighbor Directory

Your profile appears in the Neighbor Directory by default. You can opt out at any time in your profile settings. Your account stays active, but your name won't appear in the directory.

Data storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase (supabase.com), a U.S.-based database provider. We do not store payment information. Your password is hashed and never stored in plain text.

Our Facebook Page

The People's Voices has a Facebook Page. A small app we run posts to it on a schedule: a roundup of the week ahead, reminders about decisions coming up, and spotlights on campaign money and the Michigan and U.S. Constitutions. Everything it posts comes from public information already on this site.

  • The app only posts to our own Page. It is not used to sign in to The People's Voices.
  • It does not collect, read, or store information about the people who follow, like, comment on, or share our posts.
  • The only things we keep are copies of the posts we make and Facebook's ID for each one, so nothing is posted twice.

What you do on Facebook, including on our Page, is covered by Meta's Privacy Policy.

Your rights

You have the right to see, correct, or delete your data at any time. To see or correct it, email us at the address below and we'll help.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything tied to it:

  • Email chef.justinfairchild@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request", from the email address on your account if you can.
  • We'll delete your account and all associated data within 7 days and email you when it's done.

From Facebook: our Facebook app doesn't collect or store anything about Facebook users, so there is nothing from Facebook to delete. If you'd like a comment of yours removed from our Page, you can delete it on Facebook yourself, or email us and we'll remove it.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email chef.justinfairchild@gmail.com. This platform is independently operated by Justin Fairchild and is not affiliated with any government body or political party.
