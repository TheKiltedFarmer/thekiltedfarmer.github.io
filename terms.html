1. What the Hub is

The Hub is an online tool that helps small businesses keep books, figure out pay, schedule shifts, track inventory, send invoices and keep paperwork in one place. It also includes Watch alerts, the Bid & grant finder and plain-language rule explainers.

The Hub is a tool, not a professional. It is not an accountant, tax preparer, payroll service, lawyer or financial advisor. It does the math and organizes your records; you, or the professional you choose, are responsible for checking them and for what gets filed and paid. The Hub works out what people are owed, but it doesn’t withhold or file payroll taxes or send paychecks. A payroll service does that. As the employer, you stay responsible for following wage, overtime, tip and paid sick time laws, even when the Hub’s numbers help you do it.

2. Your account

  • Keep your password to yourself. You’re responsible for what happens under your login.
  • The person who sets up a business is its owner in the Hub. Owners and managers (“Stewards”) decide who else gets in and what they can see.
  • Each login can set up up to 3 businesses. Write to us if you need more.

3. Price, free months and paying

  • To start, you subscribe with a card through Stripe, our payment processor. Your first 90 days are free, and you aren’t charged during them.
  • When the 90 days are up, $10 a month per business is charged automatically to that card, and again each month, until you cancel. By subscribing, you authorize these monthly charges. Watch alerts, the Bid & grant finder and rule explainers are included.
  • We remind you before the first charge: Stripe emails you about a week ahead, and the Hub shows a reminder during your last free week. Each charge also comes with a receipt from Stripe.
  • You can cancel any time from Settings → Your Plan → Cancel subscription, in a couple of clicks, or by writing to us. Cancel during the free 90 days and you’re never charged. Cancel later and you keep access until the end of the month you’ve paid for, and nothing more is charged. Changed your mind before then? Keep my Hub on the same page turns it back on. We don’t give partial refunds for unused days, but if something went wrong, write to us and we’ll make it right.
  • If a payment fails, Stripe tries the card again and the Hub keeps working while it does. If the subscription ends, the Hub pauses: nothing is deleted, you can still download all your data, and it all comes back as soon as you subscribe again.
  • If we ever change the price, we’ll tell you by email at least 30 days before it affects you.
