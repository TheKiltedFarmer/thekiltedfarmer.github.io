The Operations Hub (“the Hub”) is run by The People’s Voices (“we”, “us”). By creating an account or setting up a business in the Hub, you agree to these terms. We’ve tried to write them so you don’t need a lawyer to read them.

1. What the Hub is

The Hub is an online tool that helps small businesses keep books, figure out pay, schedule shifts, track inventory, send invoices and keep paperwork in one place. It also includes Watch alerts, the Bid & grant finder and plain-language rule explainers.

The Hub is a tool, not a professional. It is not an accountant, tax preparer, payroll service, lawyer or financial advisor. It does the math and organizes your records; you, or the professional you choose, are responsible for checking them and for what gets filed and paid. The Hub works out what people are owed, but it doesn’t withhold or file payroll taxes or send paychecks. A payroll service does that.

2. Your account

  • You need to be at least 18 and able to agree to these terms for your business.
  • Keep your password to yourself. You’re responsible for what happens under your login.
  • The person who sets up a business is its owner in the Hub. Owners and managers (“Stewards”) decide who else gets in and what they can see.
  • Each login can set up up to 3 businesses. Write to us if you need more.

3. Price, free months and paying

  • To start, you subscribe with a card through Stripe, our payment processor. Your first 90 days are free, and you aren’t charged during them.
  • When the 90 days are up, $10 a month per business is charged automatically to that card, and again each month, until you cancel. Stripe emails you a reminder before the first charge. Watch alerts, the Bid & grant finder and rule explainers are included.
  • You can cancel any time from Settings → Your Plan → Cancel subscription. Cancel during the free 90 days and you’re never charged. Cancel later and you keep access until the end of the month you’ve paid for. We don’t give partial refunds for unused days, but if something went wrong, write to us and we’ll make it right.
  • If a payment fails, Stripe tries the card again and the Hub keeps working while it does. If the subscription ends, the Hub pauses: nothing is deleted, you can still download all your data, and it all comes back as soon as you subscribe again.
  • If we ever change the price, we’ll tell you by email at least 30 days before it affects you.

4. Your data is yours

  • Everything you put into the Hub belongs to you. You give us permission to store and process it only so the Hub can work for you.
  • You can download all of it at any time from Settings → Your Plan → Download all my data or Books → Taxes → Download all my data.
  • We never sell your data and never show you ads. See the Privacy notes for the details.

5. Using it fairly

Please don’t use the Hub to break the law, to store other people’s information without a right to, to try to get into businesses that aren’t yours, or to overload or attack the service. We can suspend an account that does these things. If we do, we’ll tell you why and still let you download your data unless the law stops us.

The Hub is provided “as is.” To the fullest extent the law allows, our total responsibility for any claim about the Hub is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the claim. We aren’t responsible for taxes, penalties, lost profits or other indirect losses.

7. If you leave, or if we stop

If you cancel, your data stays available for download for 12 months after your Hub pauses, then it’s deleted. You can ask us to delete it sooner. If we ever have to shut the Hub down, we’ll give at least 60 days’ notice so everyone can download their data.
