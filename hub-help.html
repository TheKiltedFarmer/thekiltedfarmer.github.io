Getting started

  1. Create an account with your email and a password.
  2. Set up your business: name, county, what kind of business it is, and how you pay people (regular hourly wages, or a shared pool). The Hub fills in sensible categories for that kind of business.
  3. Start your free months: add a card through Stripe. Nothing is charged for 90 days.
  4. Invite your team from Team. Each person gets an invite link. Choose who’s a Steward (owner or manager).
  5. Bring in your sales: import a Square or card-reader file, or enter a day’s sales by hand.
  6. Snap receipts as you spend, and upload a bank statement once a month to match everything up.

Each tab

Dashboard

This week at a glance, plus Coming up: bills due, overdue invoices, the next sales tax return, and schedule requests waiting on you.

Books

Sales, expenses and everything else money-related. Scan a receipt and the Hub reads it. Upload a bank statement (CSV) and it matches lines to what’s already there. Select several entries to change their category or delete them together. Bills tracks rent, insurance, licenses and renewals. Taxes shows estimated quarterly taxes, Michigan sales tax, and a year-end package for your tax preparer.

Invoices

Make estimates and invoices, pulling items from Inventory if you like. Print them, email them, send a friendly reminder, and record payments (which show up in Books as income). An accepted estimate turns into an invoice with one click.

Time Clock

Clock in and out, or enter hours after the fact. Today’s scheduled shift shows up here.

Schedule

Plan weeks or months ahead: when you add a shift, choose the days and how many weeks to repeat it. Copy last week or Copy this week ahead… fills future weeks in one go (without doubling up), and the 📅 jumps to any week. Select several shifts to change or move them together. Team members ask for time off and offer shifts for trade; owners and managers approve. Print the week or add it to a phone calendar.

Team

Who works here, their role, and their new-hire paperwork checklist (W-4, MI-W4, I-9 and the new-hire report).

Pay

Each week the Hub works out pay by your rules: regular hourly wages at each person’s own rate (set them in Team → Pay Rules), commission, or a shared pool where the week’s earnings are split by hours. Employees always get at least Michigan’s minimum wage, with overtime past 40 hours. Tips are shared fairly by hours (owners and managers can’t share, by federal law). Download a payroll CSV for your payroll service.

Inventory

Count stock with + and − buttons, or count everything at once. Edit as a table and change many items at once. Set a reorder point and get a shopping list. Add a recipe to anything you make, and “Make a batch” uses up the ingredients and adds the finished goods. Sales imports can take items out of stock automatically.

History

Past weeks and what everyone was paid.

Settings

Your business details, how you pay people, your jobs and categories, What your team can see, people and logins, your plan, and Download all my data: every table in plain spreadsheets, in one zip file.

What your team sees

Owners and managers see everything. Everyone else always gets the Time Clock, their schedule, their own pay and their paperwork. In Settings you decide whether they also see everyone’s schedule, log sales and receipts, count inventory, use recipes, or see the week’s numbers and everyone’s pay.

The tour

The first time you open your Hub, a short tour walks through every tab, with a Show me button on each step. Reopen it any time with the Help button in the corner. Your team gets their own shorter tour.

Michigan sales tax

Michigan’s sales tax is 6%. Turn it on in Books → Taxes, choose how often you file (monthly, quarterly or yearly; the Treasury tells you which), and the Hub keeps a running total of tax collected for each period, with the due date. Returns are due on the 20th of the month after each period; the annual return is due February 28. Most groceries are exempt; prepared food and most retail goods are taxable. You file and pay through Michigan Treasury Online.

If your business is in one of these cities, you generally need to withhold city income tax for everyone who works there. If you have people who live in one, you may need to withhold for them too. Rates differ by city and by whether the person lives there. The business itself may owe city tax on its profits. Tell your payroll service and your tax preparer which cities apply; they handle the withholding and filing. The Hub doesn’t calculate city tax.

Questions people ask

Does the Hub file my taxes or run payroll?

No. It figures out what everyone is owed and what you’ll likely owe in taxes, and hands that to your payroll service and tax preparer in a clean format. They file and pay. That’s why it costs $10 instead of $100.

What happens when my 3 free months are up?

Stripe emails you a reminder first, and the Hub shows one in the last week. Then $10 a month is charged to the card you added, and everything keeps working. Don’t want to keep it? Cancel before the 90 days are up from Settings → Your Plan and you’ll never be charged.

Why do you need a card for a free trial?

So your Hub keeps running without a hitch when the free months end. You’re not charged anything during the 90 days, and canceling takes two clicks.

Can I leave and take my data?

Yes, any time, even if your Hub is paused. Settings → Your Plan → Download all my data. You get a zip of spreadsheets that open in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.

Who on my team can see the books?

Only Stewards (owners and managers). Everyone else sees their own hours, schedule and pay.

I use Square. Do I still need it?

Keep using Square to take payments, including tap to pay on your phone. Download the transactions file from Square and import it in Books. The Hub adds daily sales per person, tips, sales tax and card fees, and skips anything already imported.

Can I run more than one business?

Yes. Each login can set up 3 businesses, each with its own plan. Switch between them from the top of the Hub.

How do I cancel?

Settings → Your Plan → Manage billing or cancel. You keep everything until the end of the month you’ve paid for.
