Getting started

  1. Create an account with your email and a password.
  2. Set up your business: name, county, what kind of business it is, and how you pay people (regular hourly wages, or a shared pool). The Hub fills in sensible categories for that kind of business.
  3. Start your free months: add a card through Stripe. Nothing is charged for 90 days.
  4. Invite your team from Team. Each person gets an invite link. Choose who’s a Steward (owner or manager). Just you for now? Hide the team tabs until you hire someone (see Tabs you use).
  5. Bring in your sales: import a Square or card-reader file, or enter a day’s sales by hand.
  6. Snap receipts as you spend, and upload a bank statement once a month to match everything up.
  7. Coming from QuickBooks or Wave? Bring your history, your customers and who still owes you over in a few minutes. See Switching over.
  8. Tell the Hub how customers can pay you in Settings → Getting Paid, so every invoice gets a Pay now button. See Getting paid.

Each tab

Dashboard

This week at a glance, plus Coming up: bills due, overdue invoices, the next sales tax return, and schedule requests waiting on you.

Books

Sales, expenses and everything else money-related. Scan a receipt and the Hub reads it. Upload a bank statement (CSV, OFX, QFX or QBO) and it matches lines to what’s already there. Cash ahead shows what’s coming in and going out for the next 13 weeks. Select several entries to change their category or delete them together. Bills tracks rent, insurance, licenses and renewals. Taxes shows estimated quarterly taxes, Michigan sales tax, and a year-end package for your tax preparer.

Invoices

Make estimates and invoices, pulling items from Inventory if you like. Every invoice has its own Pay now page. Send it straight from the Hub or from your own email, print it, and record payments (which show up in Books as income). The Hub shows when an invoice was emailed and when the customer opened it, and it can send friendly reminders when one is late. An accepted estimate turns into an invoice with one click.

Bids & Alerts

Included with your Hub, from The People’s Voices, with nothing extra to pay. Turn them on in this tab with two quick answers: what to watch for (your street, your line of work, licenses, zoning) and your line of work for bids and grants.

  • Bid & grant finder: each Monday, public bids and grants that fit your work, from Michigan cities, counties and the state, plus federal and national grant sites. The latest list shows right in the tab.
  • Watch alerts: a heads-up when a meeting agenda, a local decision or a news story mentions what you’re watching, and a Monday roundup of your county.
  • Explain a rule: send a new ordinance, permit requirement or state rule (a link or the text) and get back what it is, what changes for you and what to do, in plain language. A person checks every one before it’s sent.

Everything comes by email too, and every alert is kept in the tab. Only owners and managers see it.

Time Clock

Clock in and out, or enter hours after the fact. Today’s scheduled shift shows up here.

Schedule

Plan weeks or months ahead: when you add a shift, choose the days and how many weeks to repeat it. Copy last week or Copy this week ahead… fills future weeks in one go (without doubling up), and the 📅 jumps to any week. Select several shifts to change or move them together. Team members ask for time off and offer shifts for trade; owners and managers approve. Print the week or add it to a phone calendar.

Team

Who works here, their role, and their new-hire paperwork checklist (W-4, MI-W4, I-9 and the new-hire report).

Pay

Each week the Hub works out pay by your rules: regular hourly wages at each person’s own rate (set them in Team → Pay Rules), commission, or a shared pool where the week’s earnings are split by hours. Employees always get at least Michigan’s minimum wage, with overtime past 40 hours. Tips are shared fairly by hours (owners and managers can’t share, by federal law). Download a payroll CSV for your payroll service.

Inventory

Count stock with + and − buttons, or count everything at once. Edit as a table and change many items at once. Set a reorder point and get a shopping list. Add a recipe to anything you make, and “Make a batch” uses up the ingredients and adds the finished goods. Sales imports can take items out of stock automatically.

History

Past weeks and what everyone was paid.

Settings

Your business details, how you pay people, your jobs and categories, Tabs You Use, What your team can see, people and logins, Getting Paid, Moving From QuickBooks or Wave, your plan, Give a Month, Get a Month, and Download all my data: every table in plain spreadsheets, in one zip file.

Tabs you use

Don’t need a tab right now? In Settings → Tabs You Use, turn off any tab your business doesn’t use: Revenue & Expenses, Books, Invoices, Bids & Alerts, Time Clock, Schedule, Team, Inventory or History. Working alone? Tap It’s just me for now to hide Time Clock, Schedule, Team and History in one go. A new Hub with nobody else on the team offers this on the Dashboard too.

A hidden tab is hidden for everyone in your business, and the tour and the Start here list skip it. Nothing in it is deleted, and turning it back on brings everything back just as it was. Hiring someone? Turn Time Clock, Schedule and Team back on first, since that’s where your team clocks in, sees shifts and signs their paperwork.

What your team sees

Owners and managers see everything. Everyone else always gets the Time Clock, their schedule, their own pay and their paperwork. In Settings you decide whether they also see everyone’s schedule, log sales and receipts, count inventory, use recipes, or see the week’s numbers and everyone’s pay.

The tour

The first time you open your Hub, a short tour walks through every tab, with a Show me button on each step. Reopen it any time with the Help button in the corner. Your team gets their own shorter tour.

Getting paid

Set it up once in Settings → Getting Paid: a card payment link from Square, Stripe or PayPal, your PayPal.Me name, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, who checks are made out to and where to mail them. Fill in only the ones you use.

  • The Pay now page. Every invoice gets its own private web page showing the invoice and buttons for each way you take payment, with the amount filled in where the app allows it. Only someone with the link can open it.
  • Sending. On an invoice, tap ✉ Email. Send it now emails the customer from “Your Business via Operations Hub” with a View and pay button, and their replies come to you. Or choose Use my email app to send it yourself with the link already in the message. Copy the link works for texting.
  • Did they see it? The invoice shows when it was emailed and when the customer first opened the Pay now page.
  • Friendly reminders. Turn on Send friendly reminders for me and the Hub emails a polite reminder with the Pay now link when an invoice is past due: the day after it’s due, then once a week, three at most, each in the morning. You can turn reminders off for any one invoice, or send one yourself any time with Send a reminder.
  • No cut. The money goes straight from your customer to you. The Hub adds no fee. A card payment costs only what your own payment company charges, and Zelle or a check costs nothing.

When a payment arrives, open the invoice and tap Record a payment. It shows up in Books as income.

Switching over from QuickBooks or Wave

In Books, tap ⇄ Bring over from QuickBooks or Wave (or use the card in Settings). Nothing in your old program changes, and you can run it again later: anything already brought over is skipped.

From QuickBooks Online

  1. Tap the ⚙ gear and pick Export Data (under Tools).
  2. On Reports, set the dates to All dates and keep General Ledger and Profit and Loss on. On Lists, keep Customers on.
  3. Tap Export to Excel. Pick the ZIP file it makes, just as it is.
  4. For customers who still owe you, also export the A/R Aging Summary report to Excel and add that file.

From Wave

  1. Tap your business name → Business settings → Data Export → Accounting. Wave emails you a download link that works for 24 hours.
  2. Pick that file in the Hub. For who still owes you, add the Aged Receivables report too.

Before anything is saved, you see how each of your old accounts will land in the Hub’s categories and can change any of them. Bank and other balance-sheet accounts are skipped so nothing is counted twice. Open balances come in as invoices marked carried over, so when one is paid it doesn’t count as new income a second time. Something else, like a spreadsheet of your own? Any CSV or Excel file with a date, a description and an amount works.

Cash ahead

Books → Cash ahead shows the next 13 weeks: what’s likely coming in and going out each week and where your cash ends up, so you can spot a tight week before it arrives. Start from what’s in the bank today, or import a bank statement with balances. Set how much you always want on hand, and the Hub warns you (on the Dashboard too) when a week could dip below it.

It uses your own numbers: your usual weekly sales and spending, the last few weeks of pay, open invoices on their due dates, bills from the Bills tab, and sales tax returns on the 20th. If you expect a slower or busier season, tell it. Tap any week to see what’s in it, and How this is figured explains every number. It’s an estimate, so the further out, the rougher it gets.

Bank statement files

In Books → Bank, upload the statement file your bank lets you download. These work:

  • OFX, QFX or QBO (often called “Quicken” or “QuickBooks” downloads): the easiest, because they include the account and the balance.
  • CSV (spreadsheet): works with almost every bank. The Hub finds the date, description and amount columns itself.

Lines already in the Hub are matched up, nothing is imported twice, and the newest balance feeds Cash ahead.

Give a month, get a month

Settings has your own link to share with another Michigan business owner (copy it, text it or email it). When they start their Hub from your link and their first $10 payment goes through, you both get a month free: $10 comes off each of your next bills. Your card shows who you’ve sent and when the free month was added.

The link has to be used when they set up their business. It doesn’t count for another business on your own login.

Michigan sales tax

Michigan’s sales tax is 6%. Turn it on in Books → Taxes, choose how often you file (monthly, quarterly or yearly; the Treasury tells you which), and the Hub keeps a running total of tax collected for each period, with the due date. Returns are due on the 20th of the month after each period; the annual return is due February 28. Most groceries are exempt; prepared food and most retail goods are taxable. You file and pay through Michigan Treasury Online.

Michigan paid sick time

Michigan’s Earned Sick Time Act (amended in February 2025) covers nearly every employer. Businesses with 10 or fewer employees had until October 1, 2025 to comply. The Hub tracks it for you in Team → Michigan Paid Sick Time:

  • Earning: 1 hour of paid sick time for every 30 hours worked, starting the first day. Or choose to give the full year’s hours on January 1 instead (“frontloading”), which means no tracking or carryover.
  • How much: up to 40 paid hours a year if you have 10 or fewer employees, up to 72 if you have more. The Hub counts your team, or you can set it.
  • New hires: when people earn as they work, you can ask them to wait 120 days before using it. Enter each person’s first day so the Hub knows.
  • Carryover: unused hours carry over to the next year, and people still use up to the yearly limit.
  • Using it: in Schedule → Time off, choose Paid sick time and the hours. Your team asks the same way. The Hub shows their balance and won’t let a request go over it.
  • Pay: sick hours are paid at the person’s normal rate (never below minimum wage), show on the dashboard and in the payroll file, and don’t count toward overtime.
  • Written notice: every employee gets a plain explanation of your policy to read and sign in their new-hire paperwork, which the law requires.

Owners don’t earn sick time. Minors, unpaid interns, and people who truly set their own schedules are exempt. This is a summary, not legal advice. For the full rules, see the Michigan Department of Labor and Economic Opportunity’s Wage & Hour Division.

Questions people ask

Why can’t I link my bank like other apps do?

Live bank connections are run by companies that charge for every connected account, every month. That would roughly double the price of the Hub. Downloading a statement file once a month takes a minute and keeps it at $10. See Bank statement files.

Does the Hub take a cut when customers pay me?

No. Customers pay you directly through your own Square, Stripe, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App or Zelle, or by check. The Hub adds no fee. See Getting paid.

Where are the bids, grants and local alerts?

In the Bids & Alerts tab. They come with your Hub; turn them on there and the first bids list arrives the next Monday. See Bids & Alerts.

Can I bring my old books over?

Yes, from QuickBooks Online, Wave, or any spreadsheet: your history, your customers, and who still owes you. See Switching over.

A tab disappeared. Where did it go?

Someone who runs the business probably hid it in Settings → Tabs You Use. Turn it back on there and everything in it is just as it was. See Tabs you use.

Does the Hub file my taxes or run payroll?

No. It figures out what everyone is owed and what you’ll likely owe in taxes, and hands that to your payroll service and tax preparer in a clean format. They file and pay. That’s why it costs $10 instead of $100.

What happens when my 3 free months are up?

Stripe emails you a reminder first, and the Hub shows one in the last week. Then $10 a month is charged to the card you added, and everything keeps working. Don’t want to keep it? Cancel before the 90 days are up from Settings → Your Plan and you’ll never be charged.

Why do you need a card for a free trial?

So your Hub keeps running without a hitch when the free months end. You’re not charged anything during the 90 days, and canceling takes two clicks.

Can I leave and take my data?

Yes, any time, even if your Hub is paused. Settings → Your Plan → Download all my data. You get a zip of spreadsheets that open in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.

Who on my team can see the books?

Only Stewards (owners and managers). Everyone else sees their own hours, schedule and pay.

I use Square. Do I still need it?

Keep using Square to take payments, including tap to pay on your phone. Download the transactions file from Square and import it in Books. The Hub adds daily sales per person, tips, sales tax and card fees, and skips anything already imported.

Can I run more than one business?

Yes. Each login can set up 3 businesses, each with its own plan. Switch between them from the top of the Hub.

How do I cancel?

Settings → Your Plan → Cancel subscription. Stripe’s secure billing page opens with the cancel step ready; confirm it and you’re brought back to your Hub. You keep everything until your free months or the month you’ve paid for runs out, and you’re not charged again. Changed your mind before then? Your Plan shows a Keep my Hub button that turns it back on.
